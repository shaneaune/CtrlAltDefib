# Ctrl+Alt+Defib Enclosure Printing Notes

The enclosure was designed and tested using PETG.

Recommended settings:

* Layer Height: 0.20 mm
* Nozzle Diameter: 0.40 mm
* Wall Loops: 3
* Infill: 15-20%
* Supports: I used tree supports on the top edges of the RJ45 and USB holes on the front of the case. For the lid you can use supports as well and it will print 4 little discs to support the screw hole insets.  

Printing Orientation:

## Case

Print the base with the bottom surface on the build plate.

## Lid

Print the lid with the outside top surface with the lettering on the build plate. This produces a smooth outer surface and provides the best appearance for the embossed or recessed logo.
